Help & Guide
How to get the most from Vela
Getting Started

Vela tracks your personal wealth over time and helps you answer one question: are you on track to reach your financial goals? Everything is stored privately in your own Google Drive — no servers, no subscriptions.

1
Sign in with Google — your data is stored in Google Drive App Data, a private folder only Vela can see.
2
Add your first entry — go to Settings & Data and fill in your current figures: cash, ISA, pension, gold, property value, mortgage and HTB balances.
3
Set a Starting Capital — in Settings & Data, enter your invested balance at the start of your tracking period. This anchors your rate of return calculation.
4
Update monthly — add a new entry each month, including your Monthly Contribution figure (total saved/invested that month). This powers the savings rate and return calculations.
5
Add your bills — go to Bills, add each recurring commitment, and set Fixed/Variable and Essential/Non-Essential. Import via CSV if you have many.
6
Set your income — go to Budget & Savings and enter household net monthly income. This unlocks the health score and savings rate.
◎ Overview

Your financial dashboard — everything at a glance. Tap any card to see a detail sheet.

Net Worth
Total assets minus total debt. Includes property equity.
Progress to £1M
Invested wealth only (cash + ISA + pension + gold). Property is excluded because it's not liquid. The bar and estimated date update based on your 6-month trend.
Next Milestone
Shows the next invested wealth milestone (£25k → £2M) and when you're expected to reach it at your current rate.
Rate of Return
Total investment gain divided by total capital invested. Shows both overall % and annualised %. Set a Starting Capital in Settings for accuracy.
Contribution vs Growth
Of your current invested wealth, how much came from your own contributions vs market growth. Tap to see full breakdown and year-by-year returns.
Month Change / YTD
Net worth movement vs last month, and vs the start of the current year. Tap either for a detailed breakdown.
🎯 Progress to £1M

Focused purely on your invested wealth journey to £1M. Uses three trend windows (3, 6 and 12 months) to give estimated dates under different growth assumptions. The all-time trend is also shown.

Estimated dates
Based on your average monthly increase over the selected trend window, compounded forward. The more data you have, the more reliable these become.
Property section
Shown separately — property equity is part of your net worth but excluded from the £1M calculation because it's illiquid.
↕ What Changed?

A month-by-month breakdown of what drove your net worth up or down. Use the ‹ › arrows or tap any bar in the 12-month chart to navigate between months.

12-month chart
Tap any bar to jump to that month. Green = net worth increased, red = decreased.
Narrative summary
Plain-English explanation of the biggest drivers. Written automatically from your data.
What Moved
Only assets that actually changed are shown — zero-movement rows are hidden. Opening and closing net worth shown at the bottom.
Contribution vs Growth
Appears when you've logged a monthly contribution. Shows how much of that month's invested wealth change came from your own money vs market movement.
📈 Forecast

Model your path to £1M under three return scenarios. All use monthly compounding.

Scenarios
Conservative = your return rate minus 2.5%. Expected = exactly what you set. Optimistic = your rate plus 2.5%. All three use the same contribution.
Retirement age
Set your current age and target retirement age to see projected portfolio values at retirement for all three scenarios, in both nominal and inflation-adjusted (real) terms.
Inflation adjustment
Assumes 2.5% annual inflation. Real values show what your projected portfolio would be worth in today's purchasing power.
🏁 Milestones

Tracks your journey through invested wealth milestones from £25,000 to £2,000,000. Each shows a % complete and estimated date. Tap any milestone for a detail sheet showing when you crossed it or how close you are.

🌿 Financial Independence

Separate from the £1M goal. Enter your expected annual spending in retirement and a safe withdrawal rate (typically 3–4%) to calculate your personal FI number — the portfolio size that sustains your lifestyle indefinitely.

FI Number
Annual spend ÷ withdrawal rate. E.g. £40,000 spend at 4% withdrawal = £1,000,000 FI target.
FI Date
Projected using your expected return rate and current contribution, compounded monthly until your portfolio reaches the FI number.
📊 Budget & Savings

Requires income to be set (Household Income card at the top) and a monthly contribution logged in Settings & Data.

Financial Health Score
Score out of 100 based on five factors: savings rate (30%), bills burden (25%), disposable income (20%), emergency fund coverage (15%), wealth building (10%). Each is scored individually so you can see exactly what to improve.
Money Flow bar
Visual split of your income into three buckets: Bills (committed), Saved/invested, and Disposable. The percentages update live as you change income.
Savings Rate
Monthly contribution ÷ combined income. The app uses the most recent month you logged a contribution, not necessarily the latest month — so if you haven't logged June yet it will show May's figure with a label.
Category breakdown
Horizontal bars showing your bills spend by category, sorted largest first, with % of income shown next to each.
🧾 Bills

Track every recurring commitment individually. Bills are stored in your vela_config.json file on Google Drive and sync across devices.

Adding a bill
Enter the name, monthly amount, category, Fixed or Variable, and Essential or Non-Essential. Fixed = doesn't change month to month. Essential = you can't cancel it without significant consequence.
↗ History button
Tap the arrow on any bill to see its full price history, update the amount, change its type, mark it inactive, or delete it. When you update an amount it's logged with a date so you can track price changes over time.
Inactive bills
Rather than deleting a bill you've cancelled, mark it inactive. It disappears from totals but the history is preserved.
CSV import
Download the template, fill it in (Name, Amount, Category, Type, Notes), and import. Duplicates by name are skipped. Categories must match exactly: Housing, Utilities, Transport, Subscriptions, Childcare, Insurance, Food, Health, Other.
💼 Assets

Detailed view of all your assets with 12-month history charts. Tap any row to see a history chart for that asset. The portfolio breakdown shows allocation by type.

🏠 Debt & Property

Tracks your mortgage balance, LTV (loan-to-value), Help to Buy equity loan, and property equity over time.

LTV chart
Shows your LTV ratio declining over time as you pay down the mortgage and/or property value increases. Tap for a sheet showing LTV rate bands.
Mortgage settings
Set your interest rate, remaining term and end date in Settings & Data to enable the paydown projection.
Debt-to-Income
Enter your annual gross income to see your DTI ratio — useful when considering remortgaging or applying for credit.
👶 JISAs

Tracks Junior ISAs for up to two children. Child names are set in Settings & Data (JISA Settings) and saved to your config file. Data is stored alongside your main wealth data in Google Drive.

Annual allowance
The JISA annual allowance is £9,000 per child (2024/25). The progress bar shows how much of the current tax year's allowance has been used, resetting each April.
Adding entries
Each month enter the current value and how much you contributed that month for each child. The rate of return and investment gain are calculated from your total contributions vs current value.
📅 Annual Review

Year-by-year net worth history. Tap any year row to open a detail sheet showing the best and worst month of that year, total contributions, and a month-by-month table.

⚙ Settings & Data
Starting Capital
Your invested balance at the start of your tracking period. Set this once — it anchors the rate of return calculation. Without it, the first recorded balance is used instead.
Add Monthly Entry
Your main data entry form. Fill in all asset and debt balances for the month. The Monthly Contribution field (highlighted in teal) is important — it's used for savings rate, rate of return, and contribution vs growth calculations.
Edit / Delete entries
In the Data Log, tap Edit to update any existing entry without losing data. Use this to retroactively add contributions to past months.
Asset & Debt Labels
Rename any asset (e.g. "S&S ISA" → "Vanguard ISA"), hide ones that don't apply to you, or add custom assets. Changes sync to your config file on Drive.
JISA Settings
Set child names for the JISA tracker. Saved to your config file.
Mortgage Settings
Set your interest rate, remaining term and end date to power the mortgage paydown chart.
Export
JSON backup (full data export, can be reimported), Excel (.xlsx with all entries), PDF (prints current page). Keep a JSON backup before making major changes.
Themes
Five colour themes: Obsidian (dark), Chalk (light), Slate (warm grey), Parchment (cream), Midnight (navy). Saved to your browser.
💡 Tips & Common Questions
Should I include pension in the £1M target?
Yes — pension is included in invested wealth. It's part of your long-term wealth even if it's locked away. If you want to see invested wealth excluding pension, you can hide it in Asset Labels (it'll still be stored, just not counted).
Should I include Vanguard/JISA contributions in Bills?
No — they're tracked as contributions in your monthly entries and as JISA data. Adding them to bills would double-count them in the savings rate calculation.
My contribution vs growth % looks wrong
Set a Starting Capital in Settings & Data. If you've entered a large one-off contribution as a monthly figure, the spike filter (10× median) will automatically exclude it from the calculation.
Budget shows £0 saved
Make sure the Monthly Contribution field is filled in when adding or editing your entries. Budget & Savings uses the most recent month with a contribution logged.
How do I update a bill price?
Tap the ↗ button on any bill, then use the Update Amount field. The old price is kept in history so you can see exactly when it changed.
Data not syncing?
Tap the sync status pill in the nav footer. If offline, data is still saved locally and will sync when you reconnect. You can also force a sync by tapping Sync Now in the sheet.
